Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 3, 2026

Welcome to Ashu Phone. Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Ashu Phone Android application.

1. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use and the Android permissions you grant, Ashu Phone may access phone-related information and contacts to provide dialer and calling features.

If you enable call recording, the app creates audio recordings on your device. These recordings may contain private conversations.

2. Call Recordings

Call recording is an optional feature. Recordings are stored on your device in the app's recording locations. The app does not send recordings to the developer as part of its normal recording functionality.

You are responsible for using recording features in accordance with applicable laws and obtaining any required consent.

3. Google Drive Backup

Google Drive backup is optional. You can choose a folder through Android's system file picker or use the Google account sign-in option for direct uploads.

For direct Google Drive backup, the app requests the limited Google Drive drive.file permission. This permission is intended to allow the app to access files it creates, rather than your entire Drive.

Google sign-in and Drive storage are also subject to Google's Privacy Policy and terms.

If you enable cloud-only storage, the app may remove a local recording after a successful upload. If an upload cannot complete, the recording is designed to remain available locally.

4. Analytics

Ashu Phone uses Firebase Analytics for anonymous, aggregate usage events, such as app opens and feature switches.

According to the app's documented analytics behaviour, phone numbers, contacts, names and recordings are not included in these analytics events.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Google services may process information when you use features such as Firebase Analytics, Google sign-in or Google Drive backup.

Files backed up to Google Drive are stored in the Google account and destination chosen by you.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Recordings stored locally can be managed and deleted using the app or your device's file management tools. Files already uploaded to Google Drive may need to be deleted separately from your Drive account.

Uninstalling the app does not necessarily delete files previously uploaded to Google Drive.

7. Children's Privacy

Ashu Phone is a general-purpose Android utility and is not specifically designed for children.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. The latest version will always be published on this page.
